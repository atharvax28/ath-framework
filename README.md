# ATH Framework

A structured personal knowledge system that unifies tools, resources, and workflows across **cybersecurity, AI, cloud, engineering, research, and productivity** — designed for clarity and efficiency, so professionals stay organized, learn faster, and work smarter.

🔗 **Live:** https://ath-framework.vercel.app

---

## What it is

ATH Framework organizes the scattered surface area of a technical career — the tools you reach for, the resources worth keeping, and the repeatable workflows behind them — into one navigable system. Instead of bookmarks and notes spread across a dozen apps, it's a single, curated map.

## Coverage

- 🔐 **Cybersecurity** — tooling, references, and workflows
- 🤖 **AI** — models, prompts, and applied patterns
- ☁️ **Cloud** — services and deployment know-how
- 🛠️ **Engineering** — build, debug, and ship
- 🔎 **Research & Productivity** — capture, organize, and retrieve
